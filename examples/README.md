# Explore the 16 Text + Vision source images

> 🌐 Language / Ngôn ngữ: **English** | [Tiếng Việt](README.vi.md)

These images are **inputs to Qwen3.8-27B**, not pictures generated by Qwen. The collection helps explore numeric OCR, diagram-like reasoning, object recognition, everyday scenes and visual safety descriptions.

## What is included?

| Family | Count | Example tasks |
|---|---:|---|
| Numeric OCR cards | 2 | Read the printed numbers **731** and **482** |
| Rich Vision | 6 | Describe annotated 2D/3D scenes and object relationships |
| Photography | 8 | Describe food, markets, workspaces, streets and other scenes |
| **Total** | **16** | Used with 3 text-only prompts to form Full19 |

Five Photography sources are **AI-generated illustrative inputs**. Do not describe all of them as authentic camera photographs.

Each image is identified by **SHA256**, not just its filename. Source images may be 1280 × 960 or other recorded dimensions, while the inference pipeline resizes them to **512 × 384**. The source SHA refers to the **original** file, not the resized inference tensor.

## A useful example: the pedestrian crossing

The current source image [city crossing](images/photo_ai_city_crossing_1280x960.png) shows a **green pedestrian signal**, pedestrians inside a zebra crossing and vehicles behind the crossing. Its SHA256 is:

`ed532c2ba4e7eace4a81e4adfd6867c68f244fe4d64293f2bbfd1097ad7d0356`

See [image provenance and evaluation guidance](IMAGE-PROVENANCE.md). The SHA256 identifies input bytes; inference results require a corresponding TPU execution record.

## Using the examples responsibly

Check original pixels and case prompts before evaluating an answer. For numbers, verify both the value and its relation to other values. For street scenes, verify the direction and state of traffic signals. Preserve incorrect responses as part of the experiment—do not treat 19 EOS completions as 19 flawless image interpretations.
