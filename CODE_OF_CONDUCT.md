# Community code of conduct

> 🌐 Language / Ngôn ngữ: **English** | [Tiếng Việt](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.vi.md)

This project welcomes people learning about JAX, TPU inference, vision-language models, testing and open-source documentation. A respectful environment makes technical feedback more useful to everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive and precise when discussing source code, benchmarks or documentation.
- Critique technical claims and evidence, **not the people** contributing them.
- Welcome questions from people new to TPU, JAX or multimodal inference.
- Cite measurements and distinguish observation from opinion or speculation.
- Protect privacy; do not post anyone's personal data, credentials or nonpublic materials.

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, doxxing and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable in issues, discussions or pull requests.

## Raising a concern

For general moderation concerns, contact the maintainer through the repository's supported channels. For security-sensitive disclosures use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that violate these principles.

Thank you for helping create a technically rigorous and welcoming community.
